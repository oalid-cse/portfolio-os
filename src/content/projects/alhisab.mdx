## Overview

Alhisab is a scalable accounting system that supports both single-entry and double-entry transactions.

## My role

I built the backend at RetinaSoft, where I developed and maintained a full-featured Accounting Management System.

## Architecture & key decisions

- Built on Laravel and MySQL.
- Core modules: ledger, journal, reporting and financial management.
- Supports single and double-entry transactions.
- Complex financial reporting queries were optimized for speed.

## Outcomes

- Delivered a scalable accounting system with ledger, journal, reporting and financial management modules.
- Optimized complex queries for faster financial reporting.
