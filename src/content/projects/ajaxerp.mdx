## Overview

AjaxERP is an offline-first ERP system with local network sync. Its modules are HRM, Payroll and Accounting. A staging deployment is available online.

## My role

I led development of this ERP at 6amTech and designed its backend architecture.

## Architecture & key decisions

- Built on Laravel and MySQL.
- Offline-first: the system keeps working without an internet connection and syncs over the local network.
- A data synchronization strategy keeps data consistent across nodes.
- Functionality is split into modules: HRM, Payroll and Accounting.

## Outcomes

- Delivered an offline-first ERP with local network sync.
- Implemented a data synchronization strategy that ensures consistency.
