## Overview

Rebusel is a multi-vendor eCommerce platform with live chat and video calling, plus map-based product discovery. A staging deployment is available online.

## My role

I worked on the backend at RetinaSoft, where I implemented real-time chat, notifications and video calling.

## Architecture & key decisions

- Built on Laravel and MySQL.
- Real-time features: live chat and video calling between users.
- Map-based product discovery integrated into the shopping experience.
- Server and database queries were optimized to handle high traffic.

## Outcomes

- Shipped a multi-vendor eCommerce platform with live chat and video calling.
- Integrated map-based product discovery.
