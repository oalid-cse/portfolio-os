## Overview

6amMart is a multi-purpose business solution that lets a business operate with various modules, unlimited vendors and centralized products. It is distributed through CodeCanyon.

## My role

I worked on 6amMart at 6amTech, where I led development of a multi-vendor eCommerce platform and designed its scalable backend architecture.

## Architecture & key decisions

- Built on Laravel and MySQL.
- A module-based design lets one platform serve different business types.
- Vendors are unlimited while the product catalog stays centralized.
- Performance work focused on query optimization and efficient database design.

## Outcomes

- Improved performance through query optimization and efficient database design.
- Improved performance and system reliability across the platform.
